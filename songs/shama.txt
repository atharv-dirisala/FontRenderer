Beri-uko im-ikyo-koizo, fam fasal
A striped prisoner, femme fatale,
Lambi-kul kuzoe ubi unmeshi jus mil.
Gulps the pressed juice from the rabble.
Kshubi-uf zehouireb zuk ul-isho-maril,
She dances, intoxicated, with fire by night
Sal ubi nmore il-if jushoi-imo.
And is hung at the melting of the wax girl.
Nanou-ish hinm ājoih.
I despair without end.
Jāfānmu kuzo ubi hoik.
I am alone, surrounded by emptiness.
Nmi kshov-sifo-in ih hinme kabāuzo ubi ne
An easy but successful refutation after I’ve bowed my head
Haya-uf-houh sarenza.
Is an all-or-nothing surrender.
Nmari mai zuyunm-ir.
A well behaved meeting.
Bolari-maril kazoubo neish ninme.
A coercion to turn away from the negativity in you.
Hanangu ni ksharan bi kouh ksharan, shoru-ish, zāzo ubi inm ful ni,
If you call a superficial love love, unfortunately, like a fool,
Nghājo ubi nāriānm inghājo ninarāru gājāfānamāl-shashoubo.
Having said I don’t want to talk to you, I realized I was acting out of self-preservation.
Hanangu ni kshiran bi hinm nekouh, fiuvuri-kouh nmoe-unm hinme.
If you call me loving you shallow, it’s going to shatter my reality.
Nanui-ukyo-ush ke-il li fāunzeshon;
An annoying glass and foundation;
Bolā hubāri ubole ninme nmoe ni uk.
Stick out and sway your tongue like a dog.
